ABL Space Systems is an American launch vehicle company founded in 2017 by Harry O'Hanley and Dan Piemont, headquartered in El Segundo, California. It built RS1 — a two-stage kerosene/LOX small satellite launch vehicle rated at 1,350 kg to LEO — and GS0, a fully containerized ground system that packages an orbital launch site into standard shipping containers. RS1 flew once, from Kodiak in January 2023, and was lost shortly after liftoff. The company exited the commercial orbital launch market in November 2024 and rebranded as **Long Wall** in February 2025, refocusing on containerized missile defense (Cyclops interceptor, RSX boosters, Ironwood GSE).
